## 2. Decision summary

F closes by **removing unnecessary historical bureaucracy** rather than by adding a historical simulation layer.

Core result:

`DATE/EVENT-AGNOSTIC PROVOCATION → FREE FICTIONAL CONTEXTUALIZATION → NORMAL PLAYER/STORYTELLER COHERENCE`

History and canon constrain the edges of fiction; they do not drive the Provocation list.

Protected truth remains under Revelation authority.

Historical Reach remains an emergent property of biography, not a mechanical or editorial score that players must track.
