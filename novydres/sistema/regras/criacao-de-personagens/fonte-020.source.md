# J2 — Coterie Creation Architecture — Decision Ledger

## 1. Decision table

| Decision ID | Q IDs | Upstream authority | Approved decision | Downstream consumers | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `CHAR-J2-001` | Q-117 | J1; D2; E; C; Play Guidance | **Distributed correlational integration.** Coterie creation is integrated into the same character-construction trajectory. There is no exclusive coterie Marco. Whenever two or more character trajectories materially intersect within any applicable Marco, that intersection may establish or alter shared history, relationships and coterie state. Intersections may be discontinuous, with meetings, long separations and reunions. If no coterie exists, no collective layer is forced. | G, H, K, Play Guidance | APPROVED |
| `CHAR-J2-002` | Q-118 | J1; Specialist A admission policy | **Curated baseline + additional admitted options.** Novydres may present a curated ordinary baseline of coterie types while retaining other officially admitted types as additional/contextual options. Curatorial priority is presentation, not legality. J2 creates no second source policy. | G, H, K, publication | APPROVED |
| `CHAR-J2-003` | Q-119 | J1 Domain; C territorial authority | **Shared Domain with substantive territorial reconciliation.** Domain remains optional. When present, creation must establish where it exists and the local basis of legitimacy, recognition, concession, tolerance or equivalent; Domain entails both prerogatives and responsibilities over territory and kine. It is not a mere address/residence. Chasse/Lien/Portillon remain canonical and do not become Rootedness, Bairrismo, Status, Haven, ownership or Marco Territorial. | G, H, K, Play Guidance | APPROVED |
| `CHAR-J2-004` | Q-120 | J1; A; Standalone Rules; Power Scale interface | **Group ownership with contribution provenance.** Traits built as coterie traits belong mechanically to the coterie. Individual contributions may be recorded where materially relevant but do not create duplicate ownership. Narrative access does not equal individual mechanical entitlement. Material dependencies may feed Critical Dependencies. | G, H, I, K | APPROVED |
| `CHAR-J2-005` | Q-121 | D2; D1; E | **Emergent distributed shared history.** There is no separate coterie lifepath and no mandatory extra group Episodes. The same individual Episodes/Provocations can, when trajectories intersect, also create group facts, relationships, assets, obligations or consequences. No double-counting and no automatic rewriting of another member's biography. | H, G parity, K, Play Guidance | APPROVED |
| `CHAR-J2-006` | Q-122 | C; E; A/J1 legality | **Two-layer collective relationship model.** First establish fictional relation and exposure; only then mechanically represent it when an owning authority supports that representation. Distinguish group obligation, individual obligation, shared exposure and member-specific liability. No automatic conversion among them or into Prestações/Backgrounds. | G, H, I, K, Play Guidance | APPROVED |
| `CHAR-J2-007` | Q-123 | E; C Relationship Map authority | **Sufficient relational density, not complete graph.** The coterie needs enough internal relationships to explain joint operation, tensions, affinities and dependencies, but no bond between every pair is mandatory. Bonds may be direct or emerge from Played History. Membership alone does not imply trust, friendship, loyalty, debt or Ally status. E governs current 2010 relevance. | G, H, K, Relationship Map, Play Guidance | APPROVED |
| `CHAR-J2-008` | Q-124 | Power Scale; J1 separation finding | **Collective Power Profile as validation with localized correction.** The audit runs once sufficient coterie state exists. It is not canonical mechanics, XP, a dot pool or collective Power Envelope. It evaluates complementarity, coordination, shared resources, reach, dependencies, threat vector and coverage; inconsistencies are corrected locally rather than by lowering an abstract group level. | I, K, Play Guidance | APPROVED |
| `CHAR-J2-009` | Q-125 | Play Guidance; C; E; A/J1; Power Scale | **Mandatory core + conditional surfaces.** Final coterie output contains only the minimum stable group identity/state needed for play plus conditional surfaces that actually exist or matter. Domain, Haven, patron, Enemy, faction, assets, long history, Dívidas Causais and audit details are not fabricated. Unknown/Unspecified is permitted where material coherence is preserved. | G, H, I, K, Play Guidance | APPROVED |
