## 3. Decision details

### CHAR-C-001 — Rootedness at creation

**Approved option:** OP2.

Rootedness uses three conceptual stages:

1. intended/premise state;
2. qualitative trajectory evidence;
3. final validated state.

The states remain `Forasteiro → Residente → Enraizado → Histórico`.

No numeric Rootedness score and no universal fixed-year threshold exist.

Rootedness does not derive automatically from age, Generation, Blood Potency, Clan, Status, office, Marco or Bairrismo.

Rápido may use a coherent predefined trajectory; Guiado may discover/demonstrate it. Both use the same final ontology.

Rootedness and Presence State remain distinct.

### CHAR-C-002 — Local/locally formed Bairrismo

**Approved option:** OP3-A with major adendum.

Bairrismo is supernatural, real, extremely subtle, progressive and ultimately inevitable through prolonged lived exposure to Novydres.

It is not inherently beneficial.

It creates emotional/territorial dependence and a real aggravatable compulsion under its own mechanic/authority.

It uses V5-compatible Conviction and Touchstone spaces; a territorial Touchstone may be a place.

Local Cainites do not ordinarily perceive or speak of Bairrismo as a measurable condition.

Local origin or Embrace does not instantly create consolidation.

However, Enraizado + actual belonging makes consolidated Bairrismo the ordinary expected result. Lack of it in a clearly rooted character requires explanation.

The belonging milestone can be mundane and organic and must not demand extraordinary deeds, Status, office or centuries.

Histórico does not grant a second layer.

### CHAR-C-003 — Newcomer Bairrismo acquisition

**Approved option:** OP3.

Creation may internally distinguish:

`não consolidado → manifestação emergente → consolidado`

These are editorial states, not dots or required public labels.

No universal timer exists.

Trajectory, permanence, belonging and actual exposure govern progression.

Rápido may directly represent any coherent state from summarized history.

Guiado may show arrival, emergence and consolidation without receiving additional benefit.

A newcomer selecting Novydres as current residence does not receive consolidated Bairrismo automatically.

Over prolonged exposure, consolidation becomes eventually inevitable under `CHAR-C-002`.

### CHAR-C-004 — Marco Territorial selection timing

**Approved option:** OP3.

Marco is resolved during final territorial/moral consolidation after eligibility has been established.

Guiado may discover which place becomes Marco during the history.

Rápido may directly define it from coherent summarized history.

Narrative discovery does not bypass eligibility.

Marco remains distinct from Bairrismo.

### CHAR-C-005 — Raion/domain placement

**Approved option:** OP3.

Character Creation requires sufficient current territorial placement but does not convert placement into property, jurisdiction or domain.

Keep distinct:

- current raion;
- residence/refuge;
- feeding territory;
- legal/political domain;
- shared coterie domain;
- faction/institutional territory;
- Marco Territorial.

`living there ≠ hunting there ≠ ruling there ≠ owning there ≠ being supernaturally attached to there`

Rápido needs a present territorial state.

Guiado may trace movement but history does not manufacture current rights.

### CHAR-C-006 — Faction/sect/institution affiliation

**Approved option:** OP3 with adendum.

A modular internal model may represent political, sectarian, factional, institutional, Clan and juridical links.

This is not a mandatory player-facing checklist.

Absence of connection in a category is valid.

Only materially relevant relationships need survive into the final state.

Current and historical links remain distinct.

Affiliation remains separate from Status, office, Influence, Backgrounds, legal authority and territorial ownership.

### CHAR-C-007 — Personal Status at creation

**Approved option:** OP3.

Personal Status may exist at creation only when both are satisfied:

1. valid legal/mechanical allocation;
2. recognized social provenance explaining current reality in Novydres.

Narrative history explains the trait; it does not manufacture it for free.

Rápido may begin with premise-justified Status.

Guiado may discover the trajectory/Recognition but must end with a valid allocation.

External reputation does not automatically become formal Novydres court Status.

Reputação Paralela remains separate.

### CHAR-C-008 — Office and Positional Status at creation

**Approved option:** OP3.

Initial office is permitted for PCs or NPCs only as an authorized political state.

Requirements:

- real office under upstream authority;
- legal/political eligibility where relevant;
- valid provenance of appointment/election/Recognition/succession/other authorized route;
- chronicle-premise compatibility;
- Office Functional Fit;
- correct Positional Status without free Personal Status.

Rápido may receive an authorized office through premise.

Guiado may explain the trajectory but cannot manufacture authority.

Office grants only upstream-defined effects and never free supernatural power.

### CHAR-C-009 — Dignitas at creation

**Approved option:** OP3.

Dignitas may exist in the starting state of a Ventrue when trajectory and internal Clan authority support it.

It is not mandatory and is not granted merely by Clan membership.

It does not automatically derive from Status, office or power.

Creation records only the interface/provenance/current state required to support the character.

Full mechanics remain with Ventrue authority.

### CHAR-C-010 — Prestações at creation

**Approved option:** OP3.

Prestações are optional and relational.

A character may start with none, owing them, holding them, or both across different relationships.

Each active Prestação needs:

- identifiable counterparties;
- direction;
- degree where applicable;
- provenance;
- current validity;
- record/testimony when upstream rules require it.

Old settled or extinguished obligations do not automatically remain active.

Prestações do not automatically grant Status or Backgrounds.

### CHAR-C-011 — Narrative relationships and mechanics

**Approved option:** OP3 with adendum.

Narrative relationships are primary.

A relationship can remain narrative-only or become the concrete identity of a separately legal mechanical option.

No relationship automatically creates Mawla, Ally, Enemy, Status, Influence, Prestação or another trait.

The same person may participate in several independent systems without duplication.

**Adendum:** Character Creation as a whole must produce a complete sheet in both modes. Rápido must produce mechanical sheet + compatible history + Relationship Map. Guiado must use its played storyline to produce history + complete mechanical sheet + Relationship Map. Therefore downstream design must eventually set concrete values and distribution procedures even though C does not own XP or those numbers.

### CHAR-C-012 — Office Functional Fit

**Approved option:** OP3 with adendum.

Functional Fit qualitatively tests whether office requirements are satisfied by the character’s combination of personal capacity, institutional capacity, delegation, dependencies and vulnerabilities.

No universal stat/Discipline/Power Envelope floor exists by office.

The Functional Fit matrix is an **internal design guardrail**, especially for Guided storyline construction and audit.

It is not a player form.

Rápido may later expose more explicit gateways, but their exact design belongs downstream.

Access through office is not personal ownership.

A newcomer may be legitimate with valid provenance.

### CHAR-C-020 — Iskander/Zoya calibration

**Approved option:** OP3.

Iskander and Zoya are non-normative stress-test profiles.

They test whether the architecture can support highly complex social/political characters without:

- making exceptional states ordinary;
- reopening approved profiles automatically;
- creating bespoke rules for them.

1. architecture defect;
2. valid exceptional/outlier case;
3. missing provenance;
4. real upstream conflict.

The test is structural equivalence: could the final Rápido/Guiado architecture produce such a character without ad hoc rules?
