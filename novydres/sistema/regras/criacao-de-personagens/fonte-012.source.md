# F — History, Chronology & Revelation Integration

## A. Scope and authority

F defines the **minimum historical/revelation boundary** needed for Character Creation to coexist with established Novydres history.

F does not create:

- a second history of Novydres;
- a dated lifepath table;
- a historical simulation engine;
- a Historical Reach score;
- an eligibility pipeline;
- a witness/participant hierarchy;
- a player-facing canon-validation checklist;
- a new truth home.

Exactly two character-creation modes remain authoritative:

1. `Rápido`;
2. `Guiado`.

The central F principle is:

> **History constrains the edges of biography; it does not drive the Provocation list.**

## B. Upstream contracts

F consumes by reference:

- D1 for Episódio de História, Intervalo de Continuidade, chronology, retroactive insertion, Premissa Protegida and Storyteller/world authority;
- E for the distinction among historical truth, current memory, current social state, current mechanical state and causal relevance;
- B/Power Scale for age/power separation, Historical Reach, Beckoning and Historical Peak;
- C for Rootedness, Bairrismo and present social/territorial states;
- Revelation Architecture for reader access, character knowledge, truth ownership and protected resolution;
- Chapters 02–06 and Editorial Map for Novydres canon.

F does not redefine any of those authorities.

## C. Historical integration principles

1. Guided Provocations are **agnostic to specific dates and canonical events**.
2. A Provocation may be resolved in any historically appropriate context chosen by the creator.
3. The same Provocation may produce radically different histories for different characters.
4. A Provocation may refer generically to a war, rupture, migration, factional conflict, institutional decline, loss or historical transformation without naming a canonical event.
5. The creator may choose a real-world event, a V5 event, a Novydres event, a purely personal event or another compatible context.
6. Canonical history is optional fictional terrain, not a mandatory prompt pool.
7. The passage of time should become palpable mainly through **change, decay, loss, succession, obsolescence and transformation** of people, assets, institutions, identities and relationships relevant to the character.
8. Dates remain part of chronology when needed, but they are not the principal player-facing signal of elapsed time.
9. Historical coherence is a normal responsibility of player and Storyteller, not a separate validation minigame.
10. Character Creation may establish perspective about protected history, but not objective secret resolution.

## E. Historical-event usability model

Historical events are not converted into prompt types.

A Provocation may remain abstract, for example:

- involvement in a war;
- collapse of an institution;
- loss of a patron;
- political rupture;
- migration;
- allegiance choice;
- old resource becoming obsolete;
- conflict forcing a side.

The creator may then situate it in:

- personal history;
- real-world history;
- V5 history;
- Novydres history;
- another compatible fictional context.

If a Provocation produces an acquisition opportunity, such as choosing a Loresheet appropriate to a side or experience, legality and eligibility remain with the proper upstream mechanical authority.

### F.1 No mandatory taxonomy

F explicitly rejects a mandatory hierarchy such as:

- participant;
- witness;
- hearsay;
- late arrival;
- consequence-only.

The creator describes involvement freely.

No extra field is required.

## G. Historical Reach interface

Historical Reach remains an upstream concept describing actual reach into history.

For Character Creation:

- it is not assigned as points;
- it is not upgraded by age;
- it has no mandatory tier field;
- it is not tested periodically;
- it is not a universal history-knowledge score;
- it does not create a validation pipeline.

It emerges from the biography.

Player and Storyteller naturally determine what makes sense for the character.

## H. Historical truth / access / belief / player-information model

F preserves the following distinctions without demanding separate ledgers.

| Layer | Owner | May be false? | May be incomplete? | May be player-visible? | Persists via E? |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Historical truth | authoritative canon / truth home | no, once objectively closed | may be unresolved/deferred to users | not necessarily | historical truth persists independently |
| Character access | fiction + Storyteller authority | access itself is factual; content accessed may be false | yes | yes when appropriate | memory persistence handled by E |
| Character belief | player characterization within fiction; Storyteller constrains external facts | yes | yes | yes | if remembered/relevant, via E |
| Character memory | E | may be inaccurate | yes | yes | E-owned |
| Player-facing context | publication/revelation authority | may present uncertain claims | yes | yes | not an E state |
| Storyteller-only resolution | Revelation truth owner | no once established | may be intentionally withheld elsewhere | no by default | not automatically a character memory |

Core principle:

> **player knowledge does not automatically become character knowledge.**

No truth-tagging ledger is required.

## I. Protected-truth boundary

Character Creation may establish:

- what the character saw;
- what they heard;
- what they were told;
- what they believe;
- what they suspect;
- what they concluded;
- what consequences they experienced.

Character Creation may not unilaterally establish an objective resolution that belongs to a protected truth home.

Core invariant:

> **Biography may create perspective; it may not create authority over a secret.**

Presence is not a revelation bypass.

## J. Storyteller-only mediation

Storyteller mediation is **situational, not procedural**.

There is no mandatory:

- approval gate per Episode;
- `SAFE / MEDIATION REQUIRED` state;
- history-check phase;
- access audit.

The Storyteller intervenes normally when the fiction touches:

- protected truth;
- external canon;
- NPC decisions or identities outside player authority;
- institutions outside player authority;
- hidden causality;
- claims requiring Storyteller-only information;
- a material contradiction with established setting facts.

## K. 1917 stress-test boundary

The 1917 disappearance is the primary stress test.

F disposition:

1. a character may have been alive;
2. may have been in Novydres;
3. may have participated in surrounding politics;
4. may have witnessed consequences;
5. may claim direct personal experiences compatible with the Storyteller’s fiction;
6. may later hear accounts or investigate;
7. none of those states automatically grants objective knowledge of why Gilgamesh disappeared.

1917 therefore demonstrates:

`PRESENCE ≠ COMPLETE KNOWLEDGE ≠ OBJECTIVE TRUTH`

No special 1917 subsystem is created.

## L. Major-event participation eligibility

A character may participate in founding events, wars, faction conflicts, mortal conflicts, V5 conflicts or Novydres conflicts when that involvement makes sense for the trajectory.

F does not require a formal matrix calculation of chronology/geography/social proximity/rarity.

The Storyteller and player keep the character historically concise through ordinary table judgment.

The hard boundary is:

> **A generated biography does not unilaterally replace a canonical actor, contradict a closed canonical result, or make the character the essential hidden cause of an established event.**

Adjacent, subordinate, supporting, victim, beneficiary, opponent, participant and other compatible roles remain freely usable.

## M. Arrival-in-Novydres architecture

Arrival is only recorded if arrival actually occurs.

A character may instead be:

- locally born;
- locally raised;
- locally Embraced;
- already present before a relevant period;
- otherwise formed within Novydres.

No fake Arrival Episode is required.

Conceptual distinction:

`ARRIVAL ≠ RESIDENCE ≠ ROOTEDNESS ≠ BAIRRISMO`

F owns only the chronological/biographical fact.

C retains social/territorial meaning.

## N. Beckoning historical integration

Beckoning may enter history naturally when upstream authority makes it relevant.

It may fictionally contribute to:

- pressure;
- changed priorities;
- absence;
- attempted departure;
- redirected plans;
- weakened continuity;
- strained relationships;
- indirect consequences;
- resistance.

Character Creation does not:

- perform periodic Beckoning audits;
- create Beckoning checkpoints;
- create a dedicated Beckoning Provocation;
- redesign Beckoning;
- treat it as automatic removal;
- use it as a universal explanation for every absence.

## O. Historical Peak versus 2010 state

The 2010 profile remains authoritative for the current character.

Historical Peak is used only when a prior state materially explains something that still matters.

Possible historical Peak material includes:

- office;
- political power;
- territory;
- assets;
- networks;
- institutional reach;
- influence;
- other historically meaningful capacity.

Only the minimum provenance needed is recorded.

No second historical character sheet is required.

Historical Peak does not create current entitlement.

## P. Retroactive-insertion canon boundary

D1 already owns retroactive insertion.

F adds no second validation procedure.

A retroactive insertion follows the same general rules as any biography element:

- do not contradict closed facts;
- do not contradict canon;
- do not use retroactivity to create protected truth authority;
- do not silently rewrite later causality.

| Unresolved source conflict | authoritative artifacts disagree | classify `DOCUMENTARY CANON CONFLICT` | do not “fix” via character | YES, editorial |

## R. Canon-contradiction resolution procedure

There is no mandatory technical remedy order.

General rule:

> **Canon prevails; correct the smallest incompatible part of the biography and preserve as much character intent as possible.**

Possible natural corrections include:

- reinterpretation;
- changing who was involved;
- changing location;
- reducing claimed importance;
- replacing the canonical referent;
- converting certainty into belief/rumor/personal conclusion;
- keeping the personal consequence while changing its cause;
- discarding only the irreconcilable detail.

This is negotiation of fiction, not a pipeline.
