## 2. Decision lineage notes

### `CHAR-D2-001`

Supersedes the initially considered treatment of torpor as merely an ordinary continuity interval. Final approval gives torpor special fictional significance without creating a third temporal unit.

### `CHAR-D2-002`

Rejects a rigid functional family sequence such as Construction/Pressure/Rupture/Transformation as the primary prompt taxonomy. Those functions may occur in fiction, but the library's operational organization is by Marco/scope, broad construction surfaces and incidence.

The approved three-incidence structure is a D2 operationalization of D1 recurrence, not a claim that D1 had already mandated exactly three incidences.

### `CHAR-D2-003`

Clarifies "state-driven branching" as **material eligibility constraint**, not a prompt graph and not a narrative unlock system.

### `CHAR-D2-004`

Rejects automatic "gap-filling" selection that searches for a specific Trait deficit. The Alvo Funcional guides interpretation and sufficiency, while randomization preserves discovery.

### `CHAR-D2-005`

Reinterpretation opens meaning but must preserve the Provocation's core. Invalid draws and calibration vetoes create no mechanical or narrative debt.

### `CHAR-D2-006`

Adds the approved **material cruelty** requirement. Fiction-first routing cannot be used as a shield that prevents legitimate mechanical consequences from surviving to 2010.

### `CHAR-D2-007`

Keeps state manually executable. Narrative silence remains permissive; only material authority creates true blocking.
