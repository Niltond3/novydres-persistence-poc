## 4. Clarifying adenda — preserved

### 4.1 CHAR-H-001 adendum

The authorized Power Profile/envelope is defined before Guided begins. The economic/development frame already closed through G provides the resource basis. Provocations and Episodes guide where those resources become concrete on the sheet while building History at the same time.

### 4.2 CHAR-H-003 adenda

- The economic budget belongs to the chassis/architecture and Marco calibration, not to individual Provocations as mandatory catalogue fractions.
- A Provocation opens a contextual opportunity for gain, loss, transformation or mechanical construction; it does not need to expose that it is consuming or modifying hidden authorized capacity.
- No Provocation catalog must be exhausted to ensure total construction.
- Player-facing text should preserve fear and uncertainty: it must not promise that lost capacity will be recovered later.
- Individual outcomes may end below theoretical maximum due to real losses, but Guided cannot be systematically calibrated as a weaker mode.

### 4.3 CHAR-H-004 adenda

- The common Quick History structure serves as dramatic guidance for Guided Marco design.
- It must not override Protected Premise, chronology, Revelation, canon or legitimate Unknowns.
- A closed Marco is not normally reopened later because final validation discovered that its construction was incomplete; the authoring of the Marco itself must prevent that failure mode.

### 4.4 CHAR-H-005 adendum — Caderno de Trajetória

The optional Caderno may preserve raw Played History material for richer roleplay and continuity. It may be consulted when an existing mechanic makes the past relevant, but it creates no new roll, bonus, access right, memory state, XP, dot, or mechanical advantage.

### 4.5 CHAR-H-006 adendum

All migration/interoperability terminology is **internal-only**. Published user-facing text must not instruct players or Storytellers to convert, upgrade or replay an already-finished character through Guided.
