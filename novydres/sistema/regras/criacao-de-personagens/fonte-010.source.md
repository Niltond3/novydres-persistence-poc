# E — Memory, Relationships & Temporal Persistence

## A. Scope and authority

E defines how outputs of Played History are consolidated into the character's usable 2010 state.

E is not a simulation engine for the complete lifespan of every relationship, resource, identity, Touchstone or Background.

The governing principle is:

> **past explains; present consolidates.**

Both Rápido and Guiado must converge on the same ontological endpoint: a coherent 2010 character. Guiado reaches that endpoint through Played History; Rápido reaches the equivalent endpoint without simulating the persistence process.

## B. Upstream contracts

E preserves:

- D1's Episódio de História, Intervalo de Continuidade, Marcos, Pacote de Consolidação do Marco, Alvo Funcional de Construção and Dívida Causal;
- A's authority over V5 legality and mechanical status;
- B's Power Envelope and prohibition on free accumulation through age;
- C's distinction between historical relationships, current relationships and active 2010 Relationship Map relevance;
- F's authority over protected truth, Historical Reach and legitimate historical knowledge;
- D2's ownership of negative-outcome procedure, branching and prompt-state implementation;
- H's ownership of final Guided sequencing and presentation.

## C. Persistence principles

1. Historical truth and current memory are distinct.
2. Memory and canon are distinct.
3. Historical possession does not create current mechanical entitlement.
4. Historical relationship does not create current mechanical Background.
5. Mortality, replacement and transformation preserve causality where warranted, not automatic mechanical identity.
6. Long life expands possible history, not the amount of final bookkeeping.
7. The 2010 snapshot is the primary consolidation target.

## D. Historical truth vs current state

E distinguishes at least five non-collapsible questions:

1. Was it historically true?
2. Does the character currently remember it?
3. Is it socially active in 2010?
4. Is it mechanically represented in 2010?
5. Is it still causally relevant?

A single fact can answer these differently.

## E. Memory architecture

### E.1 Governing model

E preserves a selective-memory architecture without numerical capacity.

The governing relation is:

`VERDADE HISTÓRICA → HISTÓRICO VIVO / LACUNAS REAIS → RECUPERAÇÃO OU RECONSTRUÇÃO QUANDO CAUSALMENTE ACESSÍVEL`

This is not a slot system and is not a complete memory inventory.

### E.2 Verdade Histórica

`Verdade Histórica` is what actually happened once legitimately established by the proper authority.

Forgetting never rewrites historical truth, erases causal consequences or grants the character authority over protected truth.

### E.3 Histórico vivo

`Histórico` is the coherent autobiographical presentation of the character at the current construction endpoint. Rápido and Guiado must produce Históricos that are structurally comparable and visually compatible with the common Novydres character-history model.

The Histórico contains what has been consolidated and remains known, remembered or legitimately recovered enough to belong in the character's usable biography. It is not omniscient and may contain real gaps.

A fact may have been historically decisive and still be absent from the Histórico because the character no longer remembers it. Importance does not guarantee continued access.

The Histórico may grow during play when a forgotten or incomplete part of the past is legitimately recovered or reconstructed.

### E.4 Active recollection and faded/inactive recollection

`Active recollection` is what the character can presently access with ordinary narrative availability. When materially relevant, it composes the Histórico; it is not maintained as a separate numbered list.

A recollection may cease to be readily accessible without becoming historically false. Access and subjective integrity are independent qualitative ideas: a recollection may be inaccessible but relatively intact, accessible only in fragments, distorted, emotionally preserved without detail, or otherwise altered by the fiction.

These distinctions are not statistics, tracks, mandatory states or per-Episode bookkeeping. They are recorded only when their difference matters to construction, play or 2010 consolidation.

### E.5 External preservation

A forgotten past may remain externally preserved through records, witnesses, descendants, institutions, objects, places, consequences, physical traces or other carriers.

External preservation proves only what that carrier can legitimately support. It does not automatically restore subjective memory and does not bypass F/Revelation authority.

### E.6 Grilhões — optional construction note

`Grilhão` is not a separate mechanic, slot, capacity unit, mandatory field or one-to-one partner of a Memória.

At most, construction guidance may note that an object, person or place can preserve a particularly palpable causal bridge to a forgotten part of the character's past.

A useful Grilhão for a forgotten memory is normally outside the character's ordinary present access or knowledge. If the character constantly carries and consults the relevant anchor — for example, a photograph that keeps the first mortal love readily remembered — the associated material normally remains accessible enough to belong in the Histórico rather than functioning as a bridge to a genuine memory gap.

A lost sword, hidden statue, inaccessible ruin, forgotten witness, sealed record or comparable element may later become such a bridge. Rediscovery creates a possibility of access, never certainty of recovery.

### E.7 Vestígios

`Vestígio` remains a useful term for evidence or residue of a past that is no longer subjectively accessible in the ordinary way.

Examples may include letters, photographs, diaries, recordings, documents, ruins or other remnants.

A Vestígio can establish that something existed or happened to the degree its authority supports, but it does not by itself restore the original subjective experience.

## F. Memory capacity / forgetting disposition

`CHAR-CREATE-Q-061` is resolved by the corrective E decisions without a fixed numerical Memory cap.

### F.1 No numerical capacity or hidden equivalent

There is no maximum number of active recollections, no slot count, no Memory score and no sixth-memory arithmetic.

E instead uses a **qualitative-causal model**. As a life grows, some recollections may lose present accessibility or centrality, but accumulation of a new memory never automatically displaces an old one.

Qualitative saturation may identify material that is no longer being actively carried, but an actual change in access must remain coherent with the fiction or with legitimate consolidation. It is not a disguised capacity rule.

### F.2 What forgetting changes

Forgetting changes the character's **subjective access** and, where relevant, the subjective integrity of recollection.

It does not by itself:

- rewrite Verdade Histórica;
- erase causal consequences;
- delete a mechanical trait;
- remove a relationship;
- cancel an external record or witness;
- create protected-truth authority;
- create a mandatory random forgetting roll.

A materially important fact can still be forgotten. Examples include a first Touchstone, a first feeding, a defining mortal love, the reason for a centuries-long search, or the location of an important object.

Passage of time may contribute to loss of access when fictionally appropriate. So may torpor, trauma, repression, identity rupture, supernatural interference, loss of contextual anchors, extreme change or other plausible causes. No periodic check or fixed-age threshold is introduced.

### F.3 Lacunas and recoverability

A genuine memory gap may exist in the Histórico.

A forgotten recollection remains potentially recoverable while some **causal path of access** survives: a person, place, object, record, Vestígio, relationship, persistent consequence, sensory association, legitimate supernatural effect or another appropriate bridge.

The fewer or weaker such bridges become, the less plausible reconstruction may be. If the fiction establishes that no plausible subjective path remains, the gap may become effectively irrecoverable for ordinary recollection or Memoriam.

`Perdida` is not a formal state. No tracker, timer, binary flag or mandatory classification is required.

### F.4 Memoriam interface

Memoriam is the preferred V5 play interface for dramatically recovering or reconstructing a causally accessible part of the past.

A Memoriam may recover a recollection, reconstruct it partially, leave it fragmentary or reveal that part of the character's subjective recollection was inaccurate. It does not unilaterally contradict protected Verdade Histórica or create facts reserved to F/Revelation authority.

When the result legitimately establishes recovered autobiographical material, that material may enter or return to the Histórico, allowing the character's history to grow during the chronicle.

The complete Novydres Memoriam procedure — including simplified reference, full procedure, examples, trigger, declared stake/objective, scene framing, access cost/test, period-appropriate dice pools, difficulty, consequences and Storyteller-forced Memoriam — belongs in the future character-construction/system-roll appendix. E records the persistence semantics only and does not close those detailed roll mechanics here.

## G. Post-creation memory fate

The final Guided output does not preserve all construction scaffolding.

Relevant historical material ends principally as:

- the character's current Histórico, including meaningful omissions or gaps;
- current 2010 state;
- external traces or causal bridges only when materially useful;
- unresolved Dívida Causal where still open;
- Storyteller/editorial historical truth that must remain authoritative even if the character no longer remembers it.

Not every forgotten fact requires a separate inactive-memory record. When a gap itself is sufficient for play and construction, the gap is the operationally relevant surface.

Material with no remaining historical, causal, relational, memory, current-state or protected-truth function may be discarded as construction scaffold.

## H. Relationship ontology

Every relevant relationship requires only:

1. the broad nature of the counterpart;
2. a free narrative description of the bond;
3. its current temporal condition when that matters.

Broad counterpart nature may include:

- mortal person;
- vampire or other supernatural being;
- group, family or lineage;
- institution;
- place/community when the bond is genuinely social or identity-bearing.

Mechanical labels such as Mawla, Ally, Contact, Enemy or Touchstone are used only when mechanically validated.

`Figura de Trajetória` is the neutral canonical term for a person, group, family, institution or other entity relevant to the character's trajectory without automatic mechanical classification.

## I. Relationship transitions

The minimal temporal states are:

- `Ativa`;
- `Latente`;
- `Transformada`;
- `Encerrada`.

Qualities such as hostile, loving, patronal, rivalrous or subordinate are narrative qualities, not temporal states.

Transformation preserves causal continuity when the subject or form of the relationship changes.

Death is a causal event, not a universal relationship state.

## J. Narrative / mechanical mapping

Narrative relationships may become mechanically eligible when the fiction supports an appropriate V5 representation.

This is not automatic acquisition.

The internal validation chain is:

`causalidade histórica → elegibilidade → A → B → consolidação 2010`

This chain is editorial/internal and must not become a checklist for players or Storytellers.

A mechanical representation may later disappear while the historical relationship remains true.

A transformed relationship does not automatically inherit the previous mechanical status.

The V5 Relationship Map and the character sheet remain distinct surfaces: dramatic/social relevance is not identical to mechanical representation.

## K. Mortal death

Death ends the current presence of the mortal person but does not automatically erase:

- Histórico or a genuine memory gap;
- surviving subjective memory or external traces;
- optional Grilhão/Vestígio implications where materially relevant;
- causal consequences;
- transformed relationships with descendants, families or institutions.

A dead historical mortal is not automatically a current Touchstone.

## L. Descendants and institutional continuity

Continuity may pass through descendants, families, lineages or institutions only when a real causal/functioning bridge persists.

The new bond inherits history or legacy, not automatic mechanical identity.

Genealogy alone is insufficient.

Continuity may be carried by obligation, tradition, loyalty, fear, secret, property, function, promise, reputation or another concrete causal bridge.

Touchstones, Allies, Contacts, Mawla, Resources and Grilhões are not automatically inherited.

## M. Touchstone temporal validity

Played History may produce a historical candidate for Touchstone.

In 2010 the relationship remains a Touchstone only if it is still mechanically legal and currently meaningful under A/V5 authority.

Historical importance provides provenance, not guaranteed current validity.

A lost historical Touchstone may survive as:

- remembered material in the Histórico;
- a genuine memory gap whose historical truth remains intact;
- an external trace, optional Grilhão or Vestígio;
- origin of a Conviction;
- cause of another current relationship.

Historical importance does not guarantee continued subjective access.

## N. Identity / Mask continuity

Played History may include multiple identities, aliases and Masks across the past.

E does not require a complete era-by-era identity ledger.

Historical identities may explain the current state, appear in the Histórico, survive only through memory gaps/external traces, or leave consequences.

Only identities materially active and mechanically valid in 2010 may be represented as current mechanical Mask state.

Historical identities may be conceptually:

- active;
- abandoned;
- expired;
- compromised.

These are consolidation semantics, not mandatory player-facing tracking.

## O. Haven / resource persistence

Havens, Resources, property, caches, networks and similar assets are consolidated by the **2010 snapshot**.

Historical assets need not be tracked through detailed acquisition/loss timelines.

They survive in the final material only when significant because they:

- explain current state;
- belong in Histórico;
- form part of remembered history, a memory gap, an optional causal bridge or a Vestígio;
- generate a relationship or enemy;
- sustain an unresolved consequence.

Historical possession never creates current mechanical entitlement.

## P. Narrative-resource terminology

Canonical term:

`Ativo Narrativo`

An Ativo Narrativo is a useful thing the character possesses, controls or can access in the fiction without automatically being V5 Resources or another mechanical trait.

Only final-state-relevant Ativos Narrativos need survive consolidation.

## Q. Narrative-capability terminology

Canonical term:

`Competência Narrativa`

A Competência Narrativa is fictional expertise, familiarity or capability gained through history that does not automatically grant Skill dots.

Mechanical representation, when appropriate, must be validated through A/B/H.

## R. Supporting-character terminology

Canonical term:

`Figura de Trajetória`

This term is neutral and does not mechanically classify the entity.

## S. Lasting-consequence destinations

A lasting consequence survives only in the form by which it still affects the 2010 snapshot.

Possible destinations include:

- remembered material in the Histórico;
- a genuine memory gap with surviving historical/causal truth;
- an external trace, optional causal bridge or Vestígio;
- current or transformed relationship;
- current social/territorial state;
- Ativo Narrativo or its loss;
- Competência Narrativa;
- secret, reputation, obligation or narrative scar;
- validated current mechanical representation;
- unresolved Dívida Causal;
- fully closed past.

A lasting consequence does not imply permanent annotation.

`Dívida Causal` is reserved for consequences still open and causally operative.

## T. Intervalo de Continuidade persistence test

Intervalos de Continuidade do not trigger a full review of every historical fact.

The lightweight test is:

> **Did anything that matters to the 2010-building trajectory clearly continue, end, transform, or become uncertain across this jump?**

If no material persistence issue exists, nothing is added.

If one exists, only the affected element is reconsidered.

No periodic maintenance roll or lifespan accounting is required.

## U. Marco-to-Marco continuity

The Pacote de Consolidação do Marco carries forward only material state needed by later construction.

Obsolete historical detail is not required to remain active.

Later Marcos may transform relationships, memory accessibility, assets, identity or causal state without rewriting already established historical truth.

## V. Dívida Causal persistence

An unresolved Dívida Causal:

- may cross Episodes, Marcos and Intervals;
- may affect eligibility or later disruption;
- may worsen, recur, transform or resolve;
- cannot disappear merely for convenience.

If resolved before 2010, it remains separate only when its result still affects the final snapshot.

If still open in 2010, it remains a current causal surface.

## W. 2010 consolidation model

The final target is a concise character snapshot containing, as applicable:

- valid 2010 mechanical sheet;
- concise Histórico using the common Novydres history structure, with genuine memory gaps where appropriate;
- optional memory-access notes/causal bridges only when materially useful;
- current relevant relationships;
- current social/territorial state;
- current mechanical representations;
- current Ativos Narrativos and Competências Narrativas when material;
- unresolved causal elements that still matter.

The Guided mode does not need to preserve a full timeline of obsolete Touchstones, Resources, Masks, Havens or relationships.

The historical past is retained only to the degree needed to explain, enrich or threaten the 2010 character.
