# Novydres by Night — G Quick Creation Mode — Decision Record

## Decision status legend

- **APPROVED** — active decision.
- **CORRECTED** — remains active in corrected form.
- **RETCONNED / VOID** — superseded and no longer active.
- **DEFERRED** — intentionally outside G.

## CHAR-G-001 — Chassis-based Quick Creation
**Status:** APPROVED

Quick Creation uses an authorized creation chassis and explicit finite allocations while preserving the V5 trait ontology. The player sees concrete distributions rather than internal Power Envelope taxonomy.

## CHAR-G-002 — Alternative geometries by surface
**Status:** APPROVED

Relevant nonlinear surfaces use Specialist, Balanced, and Generalist distributions with equivalent economic targets. Experienced users may build directly by XP rather than take a ready array.

## CHAR-G-003 — Equivalent XP calculation
**Status:** APPROVED / CORRECTED BY LATER ACCOUNTING

Equivalent XP is calculated from the natural floor of 0 using applicable advancement costs. Unspent or returned XP is global and may be legally spent elsewhere. XP is a starting construction budget, not retrospective age-awarded XP.

## CHAR-G-004 / CHAR-G-005 — Cost from zero
**Status:** APPROVED

Traits beginning at 0 are valued cumulatively from 0 to final rating. Standard creation dots are not subtracted when calculating equivalent full-sheet value.

## CHAR-G-006 — Flaws do not grant XP
**Status:** APPROVED

Required and voluntary Flaws do not generate XP.

## CHAR-G-007 — Humanity is not age-to-XP
**Status:** APPROVED / REFINED BY CHAR-G-066

Humanity is calibrated narratively and does not become XP unless a specific rule explicitly creates an exchange.

## CHAR-G-008 — Blood Potency has XP cost
**Status:** APPROVED / REFINED BY CHAR-G-013, G-014

Blood Potency is paid normally and constrained by Generation.

## CHAR-G-009 — Global XP redistribution
**Status:** APPROVED

Global XP may be redistributed freely among legal purchases. Extreme specialization is legal when costs, caps, prerequisites, and provenance are respected.

## CHAR-G-010 — Advantages and provenance
**Status:** APPROVED / REFINED BY CHAR-G-064

XP can buy Advantages/Backgrounds heavily, but mechanically significant social, political, territorial, relational, and exceptional assets require sufficient causal provenance.

## CHAR-G-011 — Predator Type fixed package
**Status:** APPROVED / REFINED BY CHAR-G-068

Predator Type remains a fixed package and is not converted into fungible XP.

## CHAR-G-012 — Specialties
**Status:** APPROVED / REFINED

Openly purchased Specialties cost 3 XP and are part of the budget. Source-bound automatic Specialties remain nonfungible.

## CHAR-G-013 — Generation has no XP cost
**Status:** APPROVED

Generation is premise/genealogy information and costs no XP. It determines legal Blood Potency boundaries.

## CHAR-G-014 — Chassis BP is reference only
**Status:** APPROVED

The chassis provides a ready Blood Potency reference only. Actual Generation and legal BP limits prevail.

## CHAR-G-015 — Fixed chassis budget
**Status:** APPROVED

Each chassis has one fixed total starting budget. Ready configurations are calibrated against the same total and must not reward selecting one preset over another.

## CHAR-G-016 — Five active-time chassis
**Status:** APPROVED

The five chassis are Child of the Night, Neonate, Ancilla, Elder, and Methuselah. They measure active development, not chronological age.

## CHAR-G-017 — Required minimum BP is paid
**Status:** APPROVED

Generation-required minimum Blood Potency is paid fully from the chassis budget. No subsidy exists.

## CHAR-G-018 — Three allocation geometries
**Status:** APPROVED

Specialist, Balanced, and Generalist describe allocation shape rather than archetype, profession, or tier.

## CHAR-G-019 — Geometry chosen independently by surface
**Status:** APPROVED

A character may use different geometries for Attributes, Skills, and Disciplines.

## CHAR-G-020 — Geometry scope
**Status:** CORRECTED

Geometries apply only to Attributes, Skills, and Disciplines. Advantages/Backgrounds/Loresheets and Specialties are linear totals. No geometry applies to Generation, BP, Humanity, Flaws, Predator Type, Convictions/Touchstones, or derived traits.

## CHAR-G-021 — Chassis defines construction economy, not biography
**Status:** APPROVED

A chassis determines budget, reference arrays, linear totals, and BP reference. It does not automatically determine Generation, Humanity, chronological age, Status, office, or identity.

## CHAR-G-022 — Additional XP by chassis
**Status:** APPROVED

- Child of the Night: +0

## CHAR-G-023 — Global budget, no player quotas
**Status:** APPROVED

Ready arrays may be engineered using reference slices, but players are not bound to per-surface quotas. The total budget is global.

## CHAR-G-024 — Equalizer discipline

Equalizer XP should remain small: target ≤5% of the additional chassis budget; exceptional tolerance up to 10%; above 10% the preset should be redesigned.

## CHAR-G-057 — Quick operational flow
**Status:** APPROVED / CLARIFIED BY B↔G INTERFACE

Quick Creation flow begins with B's pre-sheet authorization of Power Profile and applicable exception/outlier state; only then authorize/use a compatible chassis/configuration; choose independent Attribute/Skill/Discipline geometries; apply linear totals; resolve Generation/BP; spend remaining XP; apply Predator Type; resolve Flaws; Humanity/Convictions/Touchstones; definitive History; current Novydres state; Relationship Map; localized validation.

## CHAR-G-058 — Minimum final output
**Status:** APPROVED

A completed Quick character must have complete mechanics, concise definitive History, current Novydres state sufficient for play, and a functional Relationship Map. Conditional surfaces are included only when actually present.

## CHAR-G-059-CORR — Definitive History depth
**Status:** APPROVED / SUPERSEDED STRUCTURALLY BY G-063

The final History is not 4–6 sentences. Normal target is about five concise paragraphs. Prelude is optional and never replaces the definitive declarative History.

## CHAR-G-060 — Bairrismo and Marco
**Status:** APPROVED

Quick Creation resolves current Bairrismo from the declared definitive trajectory rather than running a progression. Marco Territorial is conditional and never automatic from residence, feeding, Domain, ownership, age, XP, or Bairrismo.

## CHAR-G-060-CORR — Shared final History layout
**Status:** APPROVED AS OUTPUT CONTRACT ONLY

The final History layout is common across character creation outputs. G defines only the Quick side and the final shared layout requirement. It does not define the internal architecture of the Guided Mode.

## CHAR-G-061 — Political/social/territorial expression
**Status:** ABSORBED INTO G-064

Mechanical positions, resources, relations, and obligations are expressed through applicable Backgrounds/Advantages/Flaws and other authorized traits rather than a redundant state inventory.

## CHAR-G-062 — Minimum Flaws by chassis
**Status:** APPROVED

Minimum Flaw dots: Child 2; Neonate 3; Ancilla 4; Elder 6; Methuselah 8.

## CHAR-G-063 — Canonical five-chapter History
**Status:** APPROVED

Final History chapters:
1. A Ilusão de Estabilidade
2. A Fissura
3. A Negociação com o Abismo
4. O Ponto de Ruptura

These are narrative consolidation functions, not five new D1 procedural Marcos. G uses them for direct Quick writing. No internal Guided Mode procedure is defined here.

## CHAR-G-064 — Traits + History + Relationship Map
**Status:** APPROVED

Backgrounds/Advantages/Flaws carry the mechanical expression of positions, relations, resources, obligations, and vulnerabilities. History supplies causal provenance. Relationship Map shows currently relevant agents. No redundant parallel inventory is created.

## CHAR-G-065 — Operational Flaw rules
**Status:** APPROVED

- Flaw floor is in dots.
- No artificial cap on concentration.
- Extra Flaws do not grant XP.
- Only materially relevant and non-redundant Flaws count toward the intended floor.
- Relevant relational Flaws enter the Relationship Map when their counterpart remains active.

## CHAR-G-066 — Humanity ranges
**Status:** APPROVED

Recommended ranges: Child 6–8 ref 7; Neonate 5–8 ref 7; Ancilla 5–7 ref 6; Elder 4–7 ref 5; Methuselah 3–7 ref 4. No age-based automatic reduction and no XP exchange unless explicitly authorized by a specific rule.

## CHAR-G-067 — Convictions and Touchstones
**Status:** APPROVED

Convictions and Touchstones follow the applicable baseline and do not scale by chassis. Convictions should be operational principles causally legible in History. Active Touchstones appear in the Relationship Map.

## CHAR-G-068 — Predator Type applicability
**Status:** APPROVED

Predator Type reflects developed feeding/hunting habits. Child has none; Neonate, Ancilla, Elder, and Methuselah have Predator Type. It remains a fixed package.

## Generation / BP confirmation
**Status:** CONFIRMED — NOT REOPENED

Generation is independent of chassis. Any admitted Generation in the creation scope may be selected, approximately 14th through 4th, provided enough XP is paid to satisfy the Generation's minimum Blood Potency.

## CHAR-G-070 — Continuous localized validation
**Status:** APPROVED WITH EDITORIAL RESTRICTION / CLARIFIED BY B↔G INTERFACE

Validation checks legality, budget, causal coherence, current state, playability, and B-envelope compliance, and occurs organically during creation between Narrator and player. B's post-sheet envelope audit remains normatively required, but it does not have to appear as a mandatory checklist box. A tiny reminder may appear only if it costs no meaningful layout space.

## CHAR-BG-CORR-001 — Power authorization and construction economy are separate axes
**Status:** CORRECTIVE INTERFACE DECISION — READY FOR SENIOR EDITOR REVALIDATION

Affected B decisions: `CHAR-B-001`, `002`, `015`, `024`.

Affected G decisions: `CHAR-G-001`, `014–016`, `021–023`, `056`.

Rule: B Power Profile authorizes the chronicle's permitted power range. G chassis supplies the Quick construction economy/development template. Neither age nor chassis label determines Profile, and no chassis/preset overrides the authorized B envelope.

Existing decisions amended: none semantically; G wording is clarified at the interface.

Downstream: H/I/K must preserve the same authorization-vs-construction distinction.

## CHAR-BG-CORR-002 — Pre-sheet compatibility gate and local correction order
**Status:** CORRECTIVE INTERFACE DECISION — READY FOR SENIOR EDITOR REVALIDATION

Affected B decisions: `CHAR-B-004`, `006–011`, `015–016`, `019`, `021–024`.

Affected G decisions: `CHAR-G-003`, `009`, `014–015`, `023`, `033`, `037`, `044`, `049`, `056–057`, `070`.

Rule: before sheet allocation, authorize B Profile and exception/outlier state, then choose a compatible Quick chassis/preset. If a preset exceeds the envelope, try another preset, legal redistribution, or leave unspendable XP unused; retaining the stronger result requires B's formal gateway to a higher Profile, `Exceptional Start`, or `Declared Outlier`. If authorization is denied, correct the local excess or use another configuration/chassis.

Existing decisions amended: `CHAR-G-057` is operationally clarified so the B gate precedes chassis use; `CHAR-G-070` is clarified to include B's post-sheet audit without imposing a player-facing checklist. No G budget, array, XP cost, Generation rule, BP rule, or preset value changes.

Downstream: K must audit pre-sheet authorization and post-sheet compliance; H/I consume the same B gate.

## CHAR-BG-CORR-003 — Q-092 active lineage is Quick Character ↔ Quick Coterie boundary/handoff
**Status:** CORRECTIVE RETCON PROPAGATION DECISION — READY FOR SENIOR EDITOR REVALIDATION

Affected B decisions: none.

Affected G decisions: `CHAR-G-058`, `064`, `069`.

Rule: Quick Character Creation does not create a coterie. Q-092 remains historically registered under G, but its active meaning is the character-side boundary/handoff to a separate Quick Coterie Creation execution surface governed by J2. G may record whether a coterie already exists, character-side references/contributions relevant to later coterie construction, and consume returned coterie outputs into current state/Relationship Map. It does not construct Domain, group traits, group history, or Collective Power Profile.

Existing decisions amended: `CHAR-G-069` remains historically traceable as retconned/void for embedded coterie creation; it is not erased.

Downstream: J2 remains coterie authority; H keeps Guided ownership; I/Q-093 is untouched; K receives the separated surfaces.
