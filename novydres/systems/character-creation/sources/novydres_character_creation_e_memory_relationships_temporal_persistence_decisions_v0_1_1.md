## 4. Canonical memory vocabulary after correction

The corrected architecture uses these terms without turning them into a tracker:

- `Verdade Histórica` — what actually happened under the proper authority;
- `Histórico` — the living autobiographical presentation currently known/recovered enough to belong in the character history;
- `Memória` / recollection — subjective access to past experience, not a slot;
- `lacuna de memória` — an actual absence from usable recollection/Histórico when fictionally warranted, not a mandatory formal state;
- `Vestígio` — external evidence/residue of past material;
- `Grilhão` — optional fiction-only bridge to forgotten material, normally outside ordinary present access;
- `Memoriam` — V5 play interface that may recover/reconstruct causally accessible past material, subject to F/Revelation boundaries.

No canonical numerical progression or capacity exists.
