# I — NPC Creation Application & Depth Profiles

## A. Scope and authority

I defines how NPCs consume the already-approved common Character Creation architecture.

NPCs and PCs share one ontology and exactly two creation modes:

- Rápido;
- Guiado.

I defines only application, explicit resolution depth, omission policy, mode selection by need, audit orchestration and existing-profile revalidation.

I does not create a third mode, NPC-only ontology, NPC-only legality, NPC-only Played History engine or NPC-specific Power Scale.

## B. Common ontology / two-mode invariant

The same traits retain the same meanings for PCs and NPCs.

Differences permitted by I are limited to:

- how much state must be explicitly resolved;
- which creation mode best fits the construction need;
- how much internal audit evidence is required;
- how much of the resolved state is presented or withheld.

Resolution depth does not determine power.

Power does not determine resolution depth.

Editorial labels such as `main`, `secondary`, `legendary`, `dependent`, `official`, `recurring` or `minor` do not automatically define a mechanical profile.

## C. I application model

I uses internal production benchmarks only:

- `Integral`;
- `Abbreviated`;
- `Operational`.

`Protected / Legendary handling` is transversal and is not a fourth depth.

These terms are internal creation/audit benchmarks only. They are not player-facing or Storyteller-facing NPC categories, mechanical states, publication requirements, sheet labels or rules of play.

The governing rule is:

> Resolution depth defines sufficiency of explicit state for the intended production and operational need; it does not require symmetry or universal field completion.

A field omitted at one depth is not thereby absent from the character.

## D. NPC resolution-depth taxonomy

| Internal benchmark | Intended function | Mechanical explicitness | Narrative explicitness | History depth | Current-state requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Integral | Broad understanding, audit and reuse | Resolve all mechanically material surfaces | Broadly resolved where material | Causal depth sufficient for continuity | Full current-state coherence for intended use |
| Abbreviated | Recurring/relevant use without unnecessary expansion | Resolve mechanics needed for role and foreseeable use | Resolve identity, role, relationships and placement needed for reuse | Only causally material history | Current state sufficient for recurring use |
| Operational | Narrow operational/table function | Resolve only executable mechanics and dependencies | Minimal context needed to run correctly | Only history/provenance required by operation or audit | Current state sufficient for intended operation |
| Protected / Legendary | Transversal information-protection condition | Known mechanics remain auditable; protected extent may remain unresolved | Protected truths remain protected | History may remain intentionally incomplete | Known current state must remain coherent |

No benchmark is automatically assigned by Chapter-07 editorial label.

## E. Mandatory state by depth

The common model is resolved by function and dependency rather than by checklist symmetry.

## F. Quick versus Guided selection

Rápido is the normal path for NPC construction when the required endpoint can be established directly.

Guiado is selected when historical discovery materially contributes to building or validating the character.

NPC importance or depth alone never determines mode.

### Quick / Guided Application Matrix

| NPC benchmark | Default path | Quick sufficient when | Guided warranted when | Required validation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Integral | Quick unless discovery is useful | current state, provenance and causal coherence can be established directly | chronology, long-term transformation, protected-history interaction or causal discovery materially matters | full applicable A/B/C/E/F audit |
| Abbreviated | Quick | role, current state, relationships and required provenance can be resolved directly | the NPC's relevant role/state depends on discovering a nontrivial historical trajectory | applicable legality/current-state/power audit |
| Operational | Quick | intended operations can be resolved directly | a historical discovery process is itself needed to define the operation or provenance | limited audit of all materialized surfaces |
| Protected/Legendary | either | protected current state/envelope can be bounded directly | historical discovery is useful without resolving protected truth | protected-envelope audit + F boundary |

Q-093 is closed by this application rule.

## G. Pre-sheet Power Envelope pass

For any NPC receiving material mechanical construction:

1. establish intended function and context;
2. determine the applicable B Power Envelope / Power Profile;
3. identify Exceptional Start, Declared Outlier or other applicable exception state;
4. only then construct numerically through Rápido or Guiado.

This is orchestration of B/Power Scale, not a new NPC power system.

## H. Post-sheet Power Scale audit

After construction, audit the actual result against all materially applicable surfaces:

- Generation/BP fit;
- Discipline Depth;
- Discipline Breadth;
- Power Density / Mastery where triggered;
- Mechanical Load;
- rare/out-of-clan acquisition provenance;
- exceptional assets;
- separation of social/institutional power from personal mechanics;
- demographic plausibility;
- Beckoning/Bairrismo where applicable;
- exception/outlier state.

Integral NPCs normally receive the broadest applicable audit.

Abbreviated NPCs receive the complete audit for every resolved/materially relevant surface.

Operational NPCs receive a limited audit, but every materialized mechanic remains fully subject to its owning authority.

Localized correction is preferred over whole-character rejection.

### Two-Pass Audit Matrix

| Benchmark | Pre-sheet envelope required | Post-sheet audit depth | Power Scale surfaces required | Exception handling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Integral | yes when mechanically constructed | broad/full applicable audit | all materially applicable | formal B/Power Scale route |
| Abbreviated | yes when mechanically constructed | all resolved/material surfaces | only triggered/material surfaces, but fully audited | formal route where triggered |
| Operational | yes when mechanically constructed | limited to materialized function | every mechanic actually used plus dependencies | no abbreviated bypass |
| Protected/Legendary | yes | protected-envelope audit | known/manifested power + sufficient bounds on protected extent | mystery never substitutes for authorization |

## I. Principal NPC history depth

A principal/main NPC does not automatically require Guiado or exhaustive biography.

Historical depth is increased only when needed to explain or validate:

- continuity;
- chronology;
- current relationships;
- transformations in power or status;
- Historical Peak/current-state distinction;
- protected-truth interaction;
- major causal dependencies.

Rápido remains legal for a principal NPC when direct construction is sufficient.

## J. Secondary NPC history depth

A secondary NPC normally uses concise causal history sufficient to support:

- current role;
- relevant provenance;
- current relationships;
- social/territorial placement;
- significant facts required for play.

Secondary status does not force Rápido. Guiado remains available when discovering that history materially helps construction.

No false historical completeness is required.

## K. Legendary / Methuselah protected-envelope interface

Q-029 and Q-112 are closed by one shared decision.

Legendary/Methuselah treatment must establish enough internal evidence to prove:

- B authorization;
- plausible Generation/BP where resolved;
- plausibility of known capabilities;
- applicable exception/outlier basis;
- no contradiction between protected information and known Power Scale/Revelation constraints.

The following may remain unknown or protected when not required for operation/audit:

- exact upper mechanical limit;
- Sire;
- exact historical participation;
- full motive structure;
- complete Discipline extent;
- alliances;
- secrets.

`unknown ≠ absent`.

`protected ≠ unlimited`.

Age label alone never grants power.

The interface supports:

- ancient narrow specialists;
- broadly capable elders;
- politically enormous but mechanically moderate figures;
- dormant/torpid ancients;
- intentionally protected upper limits.

## L. Dependent / stat-block depth

`Operational` is the normal internal benchmark for dependents/stat blocks when no broader resolution is required.

Resolve only what is necessary to operate the NPC correctly, including as applicable:

- executable Attributes/Skills;
- defenses/resistances;
- manifested Disciplines/powers;
- functional resources;
- relevant relationships/dependencies;
- legality and audit dependencies.

Abbreviation does not authorize:

- NPC-only Skills;
- alternative damage rules;
- fixed-pool shortcuts outside current authority;
- monster subsystems;
- bypass of V5 legality.

A narrow Operational NPC may still be extremely dangerous.

## M. Recurring officeholder Functional Fit

Office importance does not create a universal stat floor.

Functional Fit is validated through the actual support structure available to the officeholder, which may include:

- personal competence;
- delegated competence;
- institutional resources;
- retainers;
- Influence;
- Status/authority;
- domain access;
- networks.

I does not redefine C Functional Fit.

## N. Unknown/protected data preservation

Internal state distinctions:

| Data state | Meaning | May remain unresolved? | Blocks generation? | Blocks publication? | Authority |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| RESOLVED | established sufficiently for current need | no need | no | no | owning authority |
| UNKNOWN | truth not established | yes | only if required for legality/operation/audit | not automatically | E/F |
| PROTECTED | intentionally withheld/protected by authority | yes | only if exact value is operationally indispensable | not automatically | F/Revelation |
| NOT APPLICABLE | surface does not apply | n/a | no | no | common model |
| UNNECESSARY AT THIS DEPTH | surface may exist but need not be expanded now | yes | no | no | I |
| REQUIRED / BLOCKING | current task cannot proceed without resolution | no | yes | yes for the affected deliverable | owning authority |

Unknown information stays unknown unless an authorized workstream establishes it.

I never invents hidden truth merely to complete a benchmark.

## O. Coterie/group-state handling

NPCs consume J2 without NPC-specific coterie rules.

Group state is resolved only to the depth required by:

- current membership/operation;
- material shared assets/liabilities;
- relationships;
- territorial state;
- dependencies;
- audit.

No group state is fabricated for benchmark symmetry.

## P. Existing-profile revalidation protocol

Existing approved profiles are presumed valid until material conflict is demonstrated.

Procedure:

1. triage the existing artifact;
2. assign the internal resolution benchmark needed for its current editorial/operational purpose;
3. compare the artifact against the common ontology;
4. run applicable pre/post power audit;
5. identify genuine contradiction, missing blocking state or provenance gap;
6. apply the smallest corrective patch;
7. preserve approved lore and unknown/protected information;
8. require material rebuild only when localized correction cannot restore coherence.

### Existing-Profile Revalidation Matrix

| Finding | Meaning | Corrective action | Regeneration required? | Escalation |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| PASS — NO CHANGE | sufficient and compatible | none | no | none |
| PRESENTATION PATCH | artifact formatting/presentation is stale | localized documentary patch | no | editorial |
| DEPTH COMPLETION REQUIRED | required explicit state is missing | resolve only blocking/material surfaces | no, normally | owning authority if blocked |
| POWER-SCALE CORRECTION REQUIRED | actual mechanics exceed/contradict authorized envelope | localized mechanical correction | no, normally | B/Power Scale |
| CURRENT-STATE CORRECTION REQUIRED | present-day state conflicts with authority | correct current-state surface | no, normally | C/E/F as applicable |
| PROVENANCE GAP | mechanically/narratively significant claim lacks required cause/source | establish valid provenance or remove claim | no, normally | owning authority |
| UNKNOWN PRESERVED | unresolved state is legitimate | preserve | no | none |
| MATERIAL REBUILD REQUIRED | localized correction cannot restore coherence | rebuild through Rápido or Guiado as appropriate | yes | Senior Editor |

## Q. Stress-test findings

No NPC was modified.

### Iskander Yasupov

Classification is compatible with Integral internal resolution, but existing narrative identity can be retained while mechanical finality/revalidation remains separable.

Finding: existing profile can be preserved and patched locally; no need for a new NPC system.

### Madame Zoya

Likewise supports Integral treatment with localized current Status/revelation normalization rather than regeneration.

Finding: depth and revalidation can be separated from lore preservation.

### Principal political officeholder

Representative officeholders demonstrate that role importance does not justify automatic stat inflation.

Finding: Functional Fit must consider institutional support, not only personal mechanics.

### Secondary / dependent

Leah ben Avraham demonstrates that a materially defined secondary identity may remain embedded in another profile when no independent operational need exists.

Finding: editorial presence does not imply standalone full resolution.

### Legendary / ancient

Gilgamesh demonstrates that legendary importance may coexist with partial mechanics and protected truths.

Finding: protected-envelope handling is transversal; legendary status is not a depth level and does not require secret resolution.
