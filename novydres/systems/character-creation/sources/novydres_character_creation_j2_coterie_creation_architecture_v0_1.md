# J2 — Coterie Creation Architecture

## A. Scope and authority

J2 defines one common coterie-construction architecture for Novydres, consumed by Rápido and Guiado without creating a third creation mode or a parallel ontology. It consumes canonical V5 coterie evidence from J1, social/territorial authority from C, persistence semantics from E, Played History from D2, and Collective Power Profile concepts from the Power Scale.

J2 does not redefine individual character creation, individual Power Envelopes, C's territorial/social mechanics, E's persistence ontology, D2's engine, or canonical source-admission policy.

## B. Upstream contracts

The architecture preserves:

- coterie as a real V5 group-level entity with its own shared mechanical state;
- coterie construction as associated with character creation;
- canonical coterie types, Domain, shared traits and liabilities according to admitted V5 authority;
- Domain as optional rather than universal;
- Chasse, Lien and Portillon as canonical Domain surfaces;
- C authority over Rootedness, Bairrismo, Marco Territorial, raion placement, residence/refuge, feeding/domain rights, ownership, faction/institution, Personal Status, Positional Status, office, Dignitas and Prestações;
- E authority over historical versus current relevance and Relationship Map persistence;
- D2 authority over Episódio, Marco, Provocation, eligibility, incidence, randomization, causal loss and state dependency;
- Collective Power Profile as a Novydres local audit, not a canonical coterie mechanic.

## C. Canonical/local coterie model

The coterie is a group-level entity distinct from the individual characters who compose it. Group state and individual state may interact, but ownership, entitlement and authority must remain explicit.

Core invariants:

1. group-owned trait ≠ individually owned trait;
2. individual contribution ≠ duplicate ownership;
3. narrative access ≠ mechanical entitlement;
4. shared Domain ≠ local territorial authority by itself;
5. Lien ≠ Rootedness/Bairrismo;
6. Portillon ≠ political/legal control;
7. shared Haven ≠ individual residence or ownership;
8. coterie Status-like trait/liability ≠ Personal/Positional Status;
9. coterie assets ≠ automatic individual assets;
10. collective capability does not alter individual Blood Potency, Generation, Disciplines or Power Envelope.

## D. Coterie placement in creation

Coterie construction is integrated correlationally and distributively into the same character-construction architecture.

There is no exclusive “Coterie Marco.”

Instead, whenever the trajectories of two or more characters materially intersect during any applicable Marco, that intersection may establish or alter:

- shared historical facts;
- member relationships;
- group identity or purpose;
- shared assets or liabilities;
- patrons, enemies or obligations;
- territorial state;
- other coterie state.

These intersections may be discontinuous. Characters may meet, separate for decades or centuries, and later reunite. The final coterie can therefore be the result of several historically separated correlation points.

If no coterie exists, no collective layer is forced.

## E. Coterie type admission/presentation

Novydres uses a **curated baseline plus additional admitted options**.

- A curated set of coterie types may be presented as the ordinary baseline for usability and setting fit.
- Other officially admitted types remain legal as additional or contextual options under Specialist A's source-admission model.
- Curatorial priority is presentation, not a new legality tier.
- J2 does not invent a second source policy.
- New Novydres-specific coterie types are not introduced by J2.

## F. Shared Domain architecture

Shared Domain is optional or conditionally present; it is not mandatory for every coterie.

When mechanically present, Domain is a group-owned canonical surface using Chasse, Lien and Portillon under V5 authority.

A Domain does not automatically grant each member:

- individual property;
- individual feeding rights outside group terms;
- residence;
- Haven ownership;
- Personal Status;
- Positional Status;
- office;
- Rootedness;
- Bairrismo;
- Marco Territorial.

## G. Domain/local territorial reconciliation

A coterie Domain in Novydres requires **substantive territorial reconciliation**, not merely a location label.

Creation must establish enough local state to answer:

- where the Domain exists;
- the basis by which the coterie may exercise it: recognition, concession, tolerance, legitimacy or equivalent local basis;
- the essential prerogatives attached to the Domain;
- the essential responsibilities attached to it;
- who or what can hold the coterie accountable for what occurs there.

Domain therefore represents both a territorial advantage and a burden of responsibility over a piece of territory and its kine.

This does not collapse Domain into C's local states. Those remain separately validated where applicable.

## H. Shared Advantages/Flaws

Shared Backgrounds, Advantages, group Flaws/liabilities, coterie merits and clan coterie merits are admitted only under canonical legality and Specialist A's source policy.

J2 governs architectural ownership:

- a trait built as a coterie trait belongs mechanically to the coterie;
- an individual may contribute to a group-owned trait where canonical rules permit;
- contribution does not create duplicate individual ownership;
- narrative access to a group asset is not automatic individual mechanical entitlement;
- exact dot formulas remain subject to J1 Source Pointer Debt where unresolved.

## I. Group versus individual ownership

The creation record may preserve provenance of contribution when materially useful.

Examples:

- a member may have made a shared Haven possible;
- a patron may sustain group Influence;
- one member's network may underwrite a shared Contact structure;
- an institution may be the actual dependency behind a coterie resource.

Such provenance does not alter mechanical ownership of the coterie trait. Where the coterie's capability materially depends on a member, patron, institution or asset, that fact may later surface as a Critical Dependency.

## J. Shared history architecture

Novydres uses **emergent, distributed shared history**.

There is no separate coterie lifepath and no mandatory set of additional group Episodes.

Individual Episódios and Provocations remain the construction units. When trajectories intersect, an individual result may simultaneously create:

- an individual fact;
- a shared fact;
- a member relationship;
- a coterie-state change;
- a shared patron/enemy/obligation;
- a shared asset;
- a collective consequence.

These correlation points can occur repeatedly and need not be continuous.

## K. D2 group-history interface

Shared history reuses D2 without modification.

Rules:

1. D2 Provocations remain the same open, abstract prompts;
2. no separate coterie Provocation library is required;
3. the same Provocation may be interpreted as purely individual or as individual + group-relevant when causally appropriate;
4. group relevance does not create extra Power Envelope;
5. no mechanical acquisition is double-counted;
6. one member's Episode does not rewrite another member's biography without their authorship/validation;
7. outcomes route independently to group and individual state under the correct owning authority;
8. materially incompatible group interpretations are handled by D2's existing validation rules.

## L. Shared patrons/enemies/obligations

Coterie-level external relationships use a **two-layer model**:

1. establish the fictional relationship;
2. mechanically represent it only when an owning authority supports that representation.

Possible fictional relations include patron, Mawla-like patronage, enemy/adversary, faction sponsor, creditor, debtor, institution, rival coterie or territorial authority.

Creation distinguishes:

- group obligation;
- individual obligation;
- shared exposure without identical obligation;
- member-specific liability.

No category automatically converts into another. Not every political debt is a Prestation, and a coterie obligation does not automatically create identical individual Prestações.

## M. Member Relationship Map architecture

Coterie construction requires **sufficient relational density, not a complete graph**.

The group must contain enough internal relationships to explain:

- why these characters operate together;
- what tensions matter;
- what affinities matter;
- what dependencies matter;
- what shared history remains relevant.

Relationships may be:

- established directly during conception;
- produced by correlated Played History;
- inherited from shared events;
- created by obligations, conflicts or dependencies.

Group membership alone establishes joint operation, not friendship, trust, loyalty, debt, romance, hatred or mechanical Ally status.

E governs 2010 consolidation: historically true but no-longer-relevant relationships may leave the active Relationship Map without being erased from historical truth.

## N. Collective Power Profile interface

The Collective Power Profile is a **post-construction validation audit with localized correction authority**.

It is not:

- canonical coterie mechanics;
- an XP budget;
- a dot pool;
- a collective Power Envelope;
- a replacement for individual Power Envelopes.

The audit consumes sufficient completed group state and evaluates, where applicable:

- Complementarity;
- Coordination;
- Shared Resources;
- Collective Reach;
- Critical Dependencies;
- Collective Threat Vector;
- coverage categories.

If it finds an inconsistency, correction targets the specific inconsistency rather than lowering an abstract “group level.”

Examples:

- duplicated shared/personal resources;
- extreme reach without support;
- coordination unsupported by shared history;
- unrecorded critical dependency.

Collective danger can arise from complementarity among otherwise ordinary members, while individually powerful members may still form a poorly coordinated collective.

## O. Membership-state considerations

The creation architecture can represent:

- founding member;
- later member;
- former member when historically relevant;
- rejoined member;
- task-limited or temporary membership where canon/admission supports it.

It does not create a campaign-maintenance subsystem.

Historical participation is not assumed to be continuous, and no member must have shared the coterie's full history.

## P. Final coterie output model

The final coterie state uses **mandatory core + conditional surfaces**.

Mandatory core:

- sufficient group identity/designation;
- purpose or reason for joint operation;
- current members and materially relevant membership state;
- materially relevant internal relationships;
- sufficient area/position of operation to place the group in Novydres;
- collectively owned traits/liabilities that actually exist;
- currently relevant external relationships/obligations;
- shared historical facts that still matter;
- current hooks or unresolved matters materially relevant to play.

Conditional:

- coterie type;
- Domain;
- Chasse/Lien/Portillon;
- shared Haven;
- shared assets;
- patrons/enemies;
- faction/institutional relations;
- long shared history;
- group Dívidas Causais;
- Collective Power Profile result;
- Critical Dependencies;
- other source-admitted group mechanics.

Unknown/Unspecified remains allowed when absence of data does not block material coherence.

## Q. Play Guidance handoff

Character Creation returns enough coterie state for Play Guidance to know:

- who the group is;
- what it wants;
- who belongs to it;
- where it operates;
- what it collectively possesses;
- what it collectively owes or fears;
- which relationships are currently relevant;
- what unresolved hooks remain.

No nonexistent Domain, Haven, patron or long shared history is fabricated merely to complete a form.

## R. J2→G interface

G must consume the same J2 ontology.

Required input/validation surfaces:

- group premise/purpose when available;
- intended or selected coterie type when used;
- current members;
- minimal relationships sufficient for coherence;
- shared traits/liabilities actually present;
- territorial insertion sufficient for current play;
- Domain reconciliation when Domain exists;
- mechanically relevant group relations;
- Collective Power Profile validation when materially applicable.

G may preselect or omit conditional surfaces according to Quick design, but cannot skip legality, group/individual ownership separation, Domain reconciliation, or collective-audit validation where triggered.

## S. J2→H interface

H must support:

- distributed correlation points across ordinary individual Marcos;
- no exclusive coterie Marco;
- no second group lifepath;
- same D2 Provocations;
- declaration/recognition of when individual outcomes also establish group state;
- multiple meetings, separations and reunions;
- member-specific authorship/validation;
- final coterie consolidation for 2010.

## T. J2→I interface

I receives only group-context interfaces:

- NPC membership in an existing coterie;
- group-owned versus individually owned assets;
- coterie dependencies;
- unknown group/member data;
- Collective Power Profile inputs.

J2 does not define NPC depth profiles.

## U. J2→K requirements

K must validate:

- canonical/local separation;
- source provenance;
- one coterie ontology across Rápido and Guiado;
- individual/group ownership boundaries;
- Domain collision handling;
- Relationship Map semantics;
- D2 reuse without duplicate history engine;
- Collective Power Profile remaining local;
- no group Power Envelope;
- no third creation mode;
- standalone publication without reproducing external catalogues.
