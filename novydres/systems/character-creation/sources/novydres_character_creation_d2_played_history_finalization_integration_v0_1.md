# D2 — Played History Finalization / Integration

## A. Scope and authority

D2 finalizes the Played History engine as a callable subsystem of Guided Character Creation.

D2 consumes, without reopening, D1's Episódio de História, Intervalo de Continuidade, Marco as Contrato de Construção, Alvo Funcional de Construção, Premissa Protegida, Pacote de Consolidação do Marco, Dívida Causal, autoria assimétrica colaborativa, age-scaling by Significant Coverage and provisional mechanical allocation inside B's authorized Power Envelope.

D2 integrates E persistence semantics and F chronology/Revelation authority. It does not create a competing memory model, historical-access system, social/territorial ontology, power budget, Quick workflow, Guided presentation workflow, coterie procedure or NPC depth profile.

Played History remains exclusive to the `Guiado` mode. Exactly two creation modes remain: `Rápido` and `Guiado`.

## B. Upstream contracts

### B.1 D1 — frozen construction core

D2 preserves:

- Episódio de História as the atomic played-history unit;
- Intervalo de Continuidade for compressed quiet time;
- Marco as Contrato de Construção;
- Alvo Funcional de Construção;
- three-axis Marco sufficiency: Coverage / Coherence / Consequence;
- controlled retrospective insertion;
- causal disruption;
- Premissa Protegida + Descoberta Constrangida;
- Pacote de Consolidação do Marco;
- Dívida Causal;
- no XP-per-event;
- no age-proportional Episode quota;
- more Episodes do not equal more power.

### B.2 E — persistence authority

D2 routes consequences through E and preserves:

- Verdade Histórica ≠ current recollection;
- Histórico vivo may contain genuine gaps;
- no fixed Memory cap, slots or periodic forgetting rolls;
- causal recovery/reconstruction;
- optional and non-mechanical Grilhão;
- relationship transitions;
- mortal death and descendant/institution continuity;
- Touchstone 2010 validation;
- identity/Mask continuity;
- Haven/resource persistence;
- Ativo Narrativo;
- Competência Narrativa;
- Figura de Trajetória;
- historical/current/causal distinctions in 2010 consolidation.

### B.3 F — chronology and Revelation authority

D2 preserves:

- Provocações remain agnostic to fixed canonical dates/events;
- biography may create perspective, not protected-truth authority;
- player knowledge ≠ character knowledge;
- Historical Reach is not a score;
- protected truth cannot be randomly resolved;
- canon contradiction receives minimum corrective intervention;
- Historical Peak remains distinct from current 2010 state.

### B.4 B and C

B owns legality of the Power Envelope, acquisition causality, rarity debt, Mechanical Load, Power Density and exceptional/outlier gateways.

C owns social/territorial/political state semantics.

D2 may generate causes that affect those surfaces; it does not redefine their mechanics.

## C. Final Played History engine model

The executable conceptual loop is:

`CURRENT MARCO + INPUT/PREMISE + MATERIAL AUTHORITIES`
→ `BUILD ELIGIBLE PROVOCATION POOL`
→ `RANDOMLY SELECT ONE PROVOCATION`
→ `RESOLVE ITS CURRENT INCIDENCE THROUGH INTERPRETATION`
→ `PRODUCE EPISODE OUTPUT`
→ `UPDATE ONLY MATERIAL FACTS/STATES/CONSEQUENCES`
→ `CHECK MARCO SUFFICIENCY`
→ `CONTINUE OR CONSOLIDATE MARCO`

Provocações are not nodes in a choose-your-own-adventure graph.

The engine does not search for "the correct next story beat." It selects an open question compatible with the current Marco and hard constraints. The creator discovers the answer and integrates it into the character's history.

Narrative continuity is authored interpretively and may be discovered retrospectively.

## D. Torpor / inactive-period handling

### D.1 Special status

Torpor is a special and potentially recurrent form of inactivity inside Played History.

It is not a third temporal unit parallel to Episódio de História and Intervalo de Continuidade. Elapsed inactive time is compressed through Intervalo de Continuidade. Entry into torpor, the cause of torpor, awakening, escape or material re-entry may become Episodes when they contain meaningful decision, interpretation or consequence.

### D.2 Provocation-produced torpor

Provocações may produce circumstances that the creator resolves as torpor, but Provocações should not normally close interpretation by declaring "you enter torpor."

Preferred forms establish open circumstances such as:

- being sealed;
- awakening in a crypt;
- eras having passed;
- imprisonment without ordinary access to feeding;
- re-entry into a changed world.

The creator may resolve those circumstances as torpor, conscious imprisonment, intermittent feeding, supernatural survival, mixed periods or another legal and coherent interpretation.

### D.3 Recurrent inactivity

A character may experience multiple torpor/inactivity periods. There is no universal count limit and no fixed duration.

This supports a clear distinction between:

- total chronological non-life since Embrace;
- effectively active trajectory.

A Methuselah-aged vampire may have only a few centuries of materially active trajectory. That distinction informs plausibility; it never creates an XP formula or `active years = power`.

### D.4 World continuity during absence

The world does not enter torpor with the character.

During prolonged absence, causal persistence may affect:

- mortal mortality;
- relationships;
- institutions;
- Havens and Ativos Narrativos;
- social or territorial position;
- identities;
- memory accessibility;
- Dívidas Causais;
- current availability of former opportunities.

No such loss is automatic merely because torpor occurred. E/C/B/F determine persistence and validation.

### D.5 Forbidden torpor effects

Torpor is not:

- a free reset;
- automatic forgetting;
- guaranteed power loss;
- automatic social erasure;
- XP;
- mechanical credit;
- a Blood Potency formula.

B may treat torpor as a qualitative modifier of BP trajectory only.

## E. Provocation functional taxonomy

### E.1 Primary organization

The Provocation library is organized primarily by:

1. eligible Marco / scope;
2. construction surfaces the Provocation can plausibly support;
3. incidence state;
4. only the minimal hard constraints needed for safe eligibility.

It is not organized as rigid dramatic chains such as Construction → Pressure → Rupture → Transformation, nor as dated/event families such as "1917 Prompt" or "Arrival Prompt."

### E.2 Open, self-sufficient Provocations

A Provocação is:

- short;
- self-sufficient;
- deliberately ambiguous;
- capable of being integrated without prior narrative links;
- permitted to introduce minimal new historical assumptions during resolution.

A Provocation may mention a war, companion, enemy, old relationship or prior loss even if that fact had not previously appeared in the presented Histórico. The creator then discovers what that fact meant and integrates it retrospectively, subject to D1/E/F constraints.

### E.3 Three-incidence internal arc

Every Provocation is authored with three progressive incidences of the same core:

1. **Descoberta / Introdução** — first encounter with or discovery of the core situation;
2. **Complicação / Revelação** — the established core returns with a complication, revelation or materially altered understanding;
3. **Aprofundamento / Consequência** — the core produces deeper consequence, transformation or recontextualization.

Incidences need not be consecutive.

After the third incidence, the Provocation is normally exhausted for that character unless a later approved exception explicitly permits further use.

The three-incidence rule operationalizes D1's recurrence principle without turning the whole library into linked branches.

## F. State-driven branching

D2 resolves Q-051 by rejecting narrative branching between Provocations.

There are:

- no mandatory prompt-to-prompt links;
- no narrative unlock graph;
- no microtag tree where "war=true" or "relationship=true" is required before an open Provocation may mention those elements.

"State-driven" means that current state constrains a Provocation only when a real material incompatibility exists.

A resolved Episode may affect future eligibility because it establishes or closes a fact, changes a protected premise-relevant state, exhausts an incidence, changes Marco, or invokes an upstream authority. It does not create a pre-scripted narrative path.

Continuity may instead emerge retrospectively. A later Provocation may cause the creator to recognize that a newly mentioned companion is the same figure connected to an earlier war, betrayal, love, enemy or forgotten period.

## G. Eligible-pool construction

The pool is built in this order:

1. start from Provocations authored for the current Marco/scope;
2. remove exhausted Provocations;
3. remove Provocations with true material incompatibility;
4. mark those needing authority validation as `ELIGIBLE WITH REQUIRED VALIDATION`;
5. preserve all other compatible Provocations.

Material incompatibility includes, where applicable:

- wrong Marco;
- logical impossibility;
- contradiction with a closed fact;
- Premissa Protegida violation;
- canon violation;
- Revelation violation;
- content-calibration exclusion;
- incidence exhaustion;
- another direct upstream prohibition.

Absence of a previously declared narrative fact is not, by itself, an incompatibility.

The Alvo Funcional does not cause the engine to search for a specific Trait or "missing dot." It guides interpretation and Marco closure.

## H. Randomization / selection

Default selection in Guided Played History is random among eligible Provocations of the current Marco.

The system does not create separate pools for:

- individual Attributes;
- individual Skills;
- individual Disciplines;
- specific Advantages;
- specific dots.

The Alvo Funcional of the Marco tells player and Storyteller what that phase must establish sufficiently. The Provocation supplies the open problem. The creator decides how its resolution contributes to the character.

Randomization selects the question, not the answer.

A Provocation may justify provisional mechanical expression inside B's Power Envelope, but no prompt automatically grants XP, dots or raw power.

A Marco may become eligible for closure before its Provocation pool is exhausted. Player and Storyteller may continue exploring it for richer history, but doing so grants no additional mechanical budget.

## I. Veto / reroll / reinterpretation / override

### I.1 Reinterpretation first

A selected Provocation should be preserved whenever it can be coherently reinterpreted without violating its provocative core.

Reinterpretation may widen meaning; it may not erase the situation.

Example principle:

- acceptable: "sealed for eras" becomes torpor, conscious imprisonment, intermittent feeding or another coherent condition;
- not acceptable: reinterpretation makes the sealing never have happened.

### I.2 Invalid selection

If a material incompatibility is discovered after selection, the Provocation:

- is invalid for that draw;
- consumes no incidence;
- creates no penalty or cost;
- is replaced by a new random selection.

### I.3 Calibration veto

Player or Storyteller may reject content for calibration or personal limits without fictional justification, veto currency or mechanical cost.

### I.4 Restricted Storyteller override

The Storyteller may not replace a valid result merely to force a preferred story.

Override is restricted to:

- real incompatibility;
- protected information under Storyteller authority;
- canon/Revelation safety;
- equivalent authority-based reason.

Aesthetic preference or conflict with a pre-imagined character is not, by itself, grounds for reroll.

The creator does not control the random question, but remains author of the answer.

## J. Negative-outcome architecture

### J.1 Fictional consequence first

Negative outcomes are established first as concrete fictional consequences.

A Provocation may establish or discover:

- loss;
- rupture;
- deprivation;
- sacrifice;
- destruction;
- social collapse;
- relationship degradation;
- displacement;
- identity compromise;
- reputational harm;
- moral cost;
- another adverse situation.

It should not default to `bad prompt = lose one dot`.

### J.2 Mechanical expression is real when justified

Fictional-first does not mean "mechanics are protected."

When a consequence legitimately reaches a mechanically represented surface, it may produce a real mechanical scar, subject to the authority that owns that surface.

Played History may therefore historically construct and later lose:

- Backgrounds;
- Advantages;
- Status;
- Havens;
- Resources;
- social or territorial position;
- other legally mutable states.

Humanity may also deteriorate when the trajectory and applicable V5 authority legitimately sustain that result. D2 does not create a parallel Humanity-loss formula.

### J.3 Cruelty material of the trajectory

Guided Played History preserves latent and pungent cruelty.

A developed trajectory should make it plausible to recognize not only what the character became, but what becoming and remaining that character cost.

The final concept may succeed. A creator may set out to produce a character powerful enough to serve as Sheriff and succeed. The system does not require failure of that concept.

But the trajectory may make that success bitter through:

- destroyed relationships;
- lost Backgrounds/Advantages;
- Humanity deterioration;
- Flaws;
- lost or transformed social/territorial states;
- reduced current occupancy of the authorized Power Envelope;
- lost historical peaks;
- abandoned identities;
- destroyed assets;
- causal debts;
- other material costs.

There is no quota of mechanical loss per Episode or per Marco and no punishment table.

Loss creates no refund, XP, compensation or spendable replacement budget.

### J.4 Historical loss vs current sheet

A character may once have possessed more of something than remains in 2010.

Historical possession remains true while current mechanical entitlement is validated separately.

## K. Final state-dependency engine

### K.1 Minimal dependency by material authority

Provocations need only enough state dependency to prevent true incompatibility and carry meaningful consequences.

A Provocation does not require all facts it presupposes to have been predeclared.

### K.2 Eligibility states

D2 uses:

- `ELIGIBLE`;
- `INELIGIBLE`;
- `ELIGIBLE WITH REQUIRED VALIDATION`.

"Temporarily unavailable" is represented as currently `INELIGIBLE` because of a changeable material condition, not as a separate mandatory tracker.

"Exhausted" is a specific cause of ineligibility.

"Recurring" is represented by a prior incidence with a remaining next incidence.

"Newly unlocked" is not a required narrative state. A Provocation may become eligible only when a formerly blocking material condition ceases to apply, but narrative microfacts do not create an unlock graph.

### K.3 State surfaces

The engine may consult, only when relevant:

- current Marco;
- Alvo Funcional;
- chronology;
- Premissa / input scope;
- mortal/vampire and Embrace state;
- supernatural state;
- social/territorial state;
- relevant relationships;
- memory/persistence;
- Dívida Causal;
- incidence/recurrence;
- Power Envelope;
- Revelation;
- content calibration;
- optional group/coterie context.

Not every Provocation inspects every surface.

### K.4 Local update

After an Episode, update only what materially changed or was established.

Do not generate a new tag for every fictional fact.

Shared upstream vocabulary remains the state language.

## L. Provocation authoring contract

### L.1 Conceptual template

| Field | Required? | Purpose | Authority |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Marco / scope | YES | Defines where the Provocation belongs | D1/D2 |
| Core Provocation | YES | Short, open situation/question; deliberately underdetermined | D2 |
| Incidence 1 — Descoberta/Introdução | YES | First form of the core | D2 |
| Incidence 2 — Complicação/Revelação | YES | Returns to same core with complication/revelation | D2 |
| Incidence 3 — Aprofundamento/Consequência | YES | Deepens or materially consequentially recontextualizes the core | D2 |
| Hard prerequisite | ONLY IF NEEDED | State that must objectively be true; keep rare | D1/D2 + owner |
| Hard exclusion | ONLY IF NEEDED | Material contradiction that blocks selection | D1/D2 + owner |
| Sensitive authority note | ONLY IF NEEDED | Flags canon/Revelation/calibration/mechanical gateway validation | F/A/B/C/E as applicable |
| Construction surfaces | OPTIONAL INTERNAL | Indicates broad surfaces the Provocation can plausibly inform; never dictates outcome | D2 |
| Consequence destinations | OPTIONAL INTERNAL | Possible routing surfaces after resolution | E/B/C/D1 |
| Recurrence/exhaustion | YES | Three-incidence progress and exhaustion | D2 |

This is a tabletop/book authoring contract, not a required YAML/JSON runtime schema.

### L.2 Authoring prohibitions

Do not author Provocations that unnecessarily close interpretation, such as:

- "You enter torpor";
- "You meet the first vampire you have ever seen";
- "Gain +1 Charisma";
- "Lose one dot of Resources";
- "This is the 1917 prompt."

Prefer open formulations whose factual minimum is enough to create pressure but whose cause, meaning, duration, relationship and consequences remain discoverable.

## M. Repetition / recurrence / escalation

Each Provocation owns its three-incidence mini-arc.

Incidences are not mandatory consecutive Episodes and do not point to one another by navigation.

A Provocation that has already appeared remains eligible for a later draw while:

- it remains materially compatible;
- its next incidence exists;
- it is not exhausted.

The second and third incidences should deepen the same core rather than merely repeat wording.

After the third incidence the Provocation is normally exhausted.

## N. Premise/input-scope interface

The engine receives an initial premise/input profile from the surrounding Guided workflow.

D2 requires that profile to be able to express only constraints needed for eligibility, including broad or specific protected premises.

Examples may include:

- open character premise;
- specific antiquity;
- recently Embraced;
- Sire unknown;
- Embrace circumstances unknown;
- other chronicle-authorized constraints.

H later owns how those inputs are gathered and presented.

D2 uses them only to prevent material contradiction and preserve discovery.

## O. Unknown-state handling

`UNKNOWN / UNSPECIFIED` is not equivalent to `FALSE`.

Unknown is permissive by default.

A Provocation may:

- establish a previously unspecified fact;
- introduce a retroactive fact;
- reveal that a prior assumption was incomplete;
- cause the creator to connect later material to earlier events.

Unknown becomes blocking only when the Provocation requires a certainty that cannot legitimately be assumed or when a protected premise explicitly forbids establishment.

This rule prevents premature full-sheet completion before Played History can run.

## P. Revelation-sensitive eligibility

A Provocation may create:

- perspective;
- testimony;
- belief;
- suspicion;
- investigation;
- partial or misleading subjective knowledge.

It may not independently resolve protected truth.

If a Provocation reaches a protected surface:

- preserve the Provocation through safe reinterpretation where possible;
- otherwise mark it `ELIGIBLE WITH REQUIRED VALIDATION`;
- if no legal interpretation exists, it is `INELIGIBLE`.

Random selection never grants Storyteller-only answers.

## Q. Mechanical-gateway integration

Positive Played History may justify provisional legal allocation inside B's authorized Power Envelope.

Negative Played History may reduce current occupancy or invalidate the survival of previously provisional expressions.

Neither direction creates:

- XP;
- refund currency;
- compensation points;
- age-based credits;
- power through Episode count.

If an outcome requires an exceptional or other mechanical gateway, route it to the owning authority. The Provocation itself does not authorize the gateway.

## R. Marco-completion integration

Each Marco has an Alvo Funcional de Construção known by player and Storyteller.

The Alvo defines what the phase must sufficiently establish. It does not select Traits or predetermined results.

During the Marco, the creator interprets Provocations in light of that Alvo and may consolidate provisional narrative/mechanical expressions.

Player and Storyteller jointly determine readiness for closure under D1's Coverage / Coherence / Consequence test.

A Marco becoming eligible for closure does not require exhaustion of its pool.

Continuing after eligibility adds history but no extra power budget.

## S. E persistence integration

D2 sends persistence-relevant outcomes to E rather than redefining them.

Examples:

- mortal relationship crosses a century;
- Haven may be lost during absence;
- an identity becomes unusable;
- a forgotten fact leaves Histórico vivo;
- an external causal bridge survives;
- a relation transforms or ends;
- an Ativo Narrativo is destroyed;
- a Competência Narrativa remains historically true.

Torpor and long time may contribute qualitatively to memory-access loss, but D2 adds no cap, slots, periodic forgetting roll or formal Lost-memory state.

## T. F chronology/revelation integration

D2 allows controlled retrospective integration because open Provocations may establish previously unstated historical facts.

Such integration may:

- explain;
- add cause;
- recontextualize;
- fill a compressed period;
- connect two previously separate Episodes.

It may not:

- contradict closed facts;
- violate Premissa Protegida;
- contradict canon;
- resolve protected truth;
- convert participation into objective knowledge.

Canonical history constrains biography but is not the Provocation catalogue.

## U. Final Played History output package

D2 returns to H a package capable of carrying:

- chronology summary;
- Marco consolidation packages;
- materially important Episodes;
- current social/territorial state;
- current relationships;
- historical relationships still materially relevant;
- Histórico vivo and meaningful gaps;
- persistence-relevant bridges only where useful;
- provisional/final mechanical allocations and causal justifications;
- acquisitions and losses relevant to current legality;
- remaining Dívidas Causais;
- identity/Mask and asset history/current state;
- historical facts relevant to F;
- historical peaks where still useful as history;
- current 2010 endpoint;
- unresolved validation surfaces.

The output must keep distinguishable:

- historically true;
- currently remembered;
- currently socially active;
- currently mechanically represented;
- causally unresolved.

Current 2010 state is the endpoint; Historical Peak is not a second current sheet.

## V. D2→G compatibility

G does not run Played History.

To reach an equivalent legal 2010 endpoint, Quick must be capable of producing or validating the same classes of endpoint information:

- legal current mechanical state;
- current social/territorial minimum required by C;
- current relationships sufficient for the V5 Relationship Map;
- current Humanity / Convictions / Touchstone legality as owned upstream;
- identity/Mask and current assets as applicable;
- coherent historical explanation sufficient for current state;
- any material historical facts or Dívidas Causais that the character concept requires;
- distinction between current state and any historical peak;
- no assumption that omitted Played History grants extra power;
- no Memory-cap regression.

Quick need not reproduce Episodes, Provocation incidence or the Guided discovery process.

## W. D2→H interface

H receives a callable subsystem with:

### Entry requirements

- authorized creation premise/profile;
- current Marco;
- its Alvo Funcional;
- initial protected premise/input scope;
- applicable Power Envelope;
- upstream states already known;
- calibration limits.

### Engine inputs

- Provocation library for the current Marco;
- incidence/exhaustion state;
- material state surfaces;
- upstream authority constraints.

### Execution loop

1. construct eligible pool;
2. randomize;
3. reinterpret/validate if needed;
4. resolve current incidence as Episódio;
5. route persistence/mechanical/social/chronology consequences;
6. update material state only;
7. apply D1 Marco sufficiency;
8. continue or consolidate.

### Termination

Played History terminates when all required Marcos are consolidated and the final 2010 endpoint is ready for H's final Guided validation.

### Final package

Use section U.

### Unresolved validation surfaces

H may still need to coordinate final sheet presentation, final legality checks and any owner-specific pending validation.

D2 does not design H's player-facing sequence or UI/book layout.

## X. D2→J2 interface

J2 may supply optional group context to generic D2 surfaces, including:

- shared historical facts;
- shared patrons/enemies;
- group/coterie state;
- Relationship Map context;
- Domain/shared assets;
- group-linked Dívidas Causais.

D2 can consume those as premise/state/context constraints where materially relevant.

D2 does not author group Provocations, shared Played History rules or coterie workflow.

A character need not have a coterie for D2 to function.

## Y. D2→I interface

NPCs use the same ontology.

### Ontologically required endpoint classes

Where applicable to the NPC:

- coherent current 2010 state;
- legal current mechanical state;
- minimum social/territorial state;
- materially relevant current relationships;
- identity/assets where relevant;
- historically sufficient causes for current state;
- unresolved consequences that remain materially active.

### Optionally abstractable

I may later abstract:

- number of Episodes;
- most Provocation text;
- intermediate Marco detail;
- old inactive relationships;
- obsolete assets;
- minor historical peaks;
- non-material memory detail.

### Potentially unknown

I may preserve unknown:

- Sire details;
- parts of history;
- protected truth;
- subjective memory gaps;
- concealed affiliations or relationships;
- other information legitimately unavailable at the selected NPC depth.

D2 does not define NPC depth profiles.

## Z. D2→K requirements

K must preserve:

- one common character ontology;
- exactly two creation modes;
- Played History belongs to Guided;
- Quick endpoint parity;
- no XP-per-event;
- no age=power;
- no age-proportional Episode quota;

- random selection after eligibility;

- causal losses;

- current 2010 state distinct from Historical Peak;
- authoring metadata remains internal unless publication genuinely needs it.
