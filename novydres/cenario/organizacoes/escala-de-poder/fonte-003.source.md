## 3. Complete drill ledger — consolidated results

### 3.1 Demographic and chronological architecture

`NPC-POWER-001` through `006`, `033` through `044`, `052`, `053`, `062`, `063`, `074` through `085` establish a city whose active resident population is younger than its history, with a broad young base, an Ancilla center, a narrow active elder layer, and individually audited outliers.

The Beckoning, Torpor, withdrawal, death, and other losses explain why historical depth does not equal active elder saturation.

Rootedness is independent from age and power. Thin-Bloods and Caitiff are both demographically rare in Novydres, and their combined presence outside ordinary clan structures receives an additional citywide audit.

### 3.2 Generation, Blood Potency, Disciplines, and mechanics

`NPC-POWER-004` through `012`, `039`, `040`, `047` through `051`, `067`, `068`, `096` through `103` establish the anti-power-creep core:

- Generation is genealogical.
- BP follows Generation limits and individual trajectory.
- Disciplines require Depth, Breadth, Mastery, and Density audit.
- Rare acquisition needs a path.
- Age is not retrospective XP.
- Atributos, Perícias, Especializações, Vantagens, Antecedentes, Willpower, and other mechanics do not inflate automatically with centuries.
- Mechanical Load detects a sheet that is excessive by total concentration even if no one trait is individually absurd.
- Weaknesses and Flaws do not buy exceptional power.

### 3.3 Political, social, institutional, and territorial power

`NPC-POWER-013` through `015`, `035` through `037`, `045`, `046`, `057` through `061`, `070`, `071`, `086` through `089`, `104`, `105` separate personal power from:

- office;
- jurisdiction;
- resources;
- mobilizable organizations;
- territorial advantage;
- social leverage;
- historical leverage;
- collective power;
- reputation.

Power must have source, dependencies, constraints, and when useful, counterplay.

### 3.4 Bairrismo and Beckoning

`NPC-POWER-016` through `032`, `081` establish:

- Bairrismo as territorial Conviction/anchoring architecture rather than a numeric stat;
- Beckoning eligibility by Generation and intensity by BP;
- finite triggered duration;
- no resistance trait;
- no automatic Bairrismo resistance bonus;
- diablerie as temporary suppression with victim-BP-based duration and major consequence burden.

### 3.5 Outliers, rarity, and citywide governance

`NPC-POWER-038` through `044`, `056`, `064` through `080`, `082` through `087` establish:

- Declared Outliers;
- qualitative exception classes;
- survival funnel;
- local clan identity precedence;
- Local Divergence categories;
- Power vs Trajectory Exception;
- Functional Saturation;
- Citywide Exception Register;
- Exception Density Review;
- Collective Power Profile and Collective Coverage.

### 3.6 Historical, moral, and epistemic coherence

`NPC-POWER-014`, `050`, `077`, `078`, `088` through `090` establish:

- Historical Reach independent of age;
- knowledge can be operational, restricted, fragmentary, or lost;
- Unknown stays Unknown;
- revelation-protected legendary envelopes;
- Perception Gap between actual and perceived capability;
- 2010 current profile distinguished from Historical Peak;
- Humanity remains a separate moral dimension rather than a power metric.

### 3.7 Governance and future character creation

`NPC-POWER-091` through `095`, `099`, `100`, `106` establish a new mandatory workstream:

`character_creation_novydres_architecture`

This next gate must close before Chapter 07 NPC generation resumes.

The Power Scale may be READY FOR SENIOR EDITOR REVALIDATION while NPC generation remains blocked.

The future workstream receives an Integration Contract but retains authority to decide its own workflow, modes, metrics, point distribution, templates, guided creation, and PJ/NPC-specific construction methods.

## 4. Shared Character Invariants

The following invariants apply to both PCs and NPCs unless a future approved rule explicitly creates a valid exception:

1. Generation ≠ age ≠ Blood Potency.
2. Time creates opportunity, not automatic dots.
3. Exceptional mechanics require acquisition and trajectory.
4. Resources require origin, maintenance, and vulnerability.
5. Institutional authority is not personal mechanical strength.
6. Rootedness and Bairrismo are not functions of age or BP.
7. Historical depth does not equal omniscience.
8. Weaknesses do not pay for exceptional power.
9. Power should have a defining operational identity and at least one dependency/constraint.
10. Sheet mechanics should emerge from character trajectory rather than be retroactively justified by biography.

## 5. NPC-only citywide controls

The following remain setting/NPC-side controls and must not automatically become PC-creation restrictions:

- demographic pyramid;
- elder/Ancillae ratio alarms;
- citywide rarity distribution;

- Exception Density Review;
- Functional Saturation;
- clan/raion population asymmetry audit;
- active vs absent/dormant demographic accounting;
- benchmark audit of existing Dramatis Personae;
